# Copy rules, store specs and review checklist

## Headlines
- One idea per screenshot. The first 3 screenshots carry the most weight: they show in search results at thumbnail size.
- 2–6 words in `headline` + 1–3 key words in `accent`. Direct, service-focused, named actions ("Send parcels across the UAE." beats "Joy in every mile.").
- Mirror the language users search for (from the listing description, keyword research, competitor titles).
- Tell a task end to end across the set (e.g. book → pay → track → delivered).
- Two or three features per screen at most. Don't misrepresent the UI.
- No unverifiable claims: prices, times, ratings, "#1", awards — only if the client confirms.

## Store sizes the builder outputs
| Store | Asset | Size |
|---|---|---|
| App Store | iPhone 6.9″ screenshots | 1290 × 2796 |
| App Store | iPhone 6.5″ screenshots | 1242 × 2688 |
| Google Play | Phone screenshots | 1080 × 1920 (9:16; Play allows at most 2:1 on the long side) |
| Google Play | Landscape lead screenshot | 1920 × 1080 |
| Google Play | Feature graphic | 1024 × 500 |

Up to 10 screenshots on the App Store and 8 per device type on Google Play. iOS has no landscape lead: the set starts at the first portrait.

## Review checklist before handing over
- No headline overlaps the phone; no clipped text.
- Crop cards sit over the matching UI element and read clearly.
- Real screens: clean status bar (full battery, 9:41), no personal data, no error / returned states, high resolution (≥1080 px wide).
- Logo on the first frame; brand colours correct; accent words carry the key message.
- Every figure on a card or screen is real and approved.
- Photos are licensed or owned by the client (no watermarked stock).
